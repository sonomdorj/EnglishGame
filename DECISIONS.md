# DECISIONS

1. The repo had an older skeleton (a different word game). PLAN.md was replaced with "The Name Keeper" spec. Old files `game.js`, `words.js`, `style.css` were removed. `style.css` was written new.
2. Three.js 0.160.0 from jsDelivr via an import map. No addons. Own pointer-lock look code.
3. Port 3010 for `npx serve`. Port 3000 is never used.
4. Browser testing: Playwright with the pre-installed Chromium. The test sandbox blocks the CDN, so tests serve the same three.js file from a local npm copy. The game itself still loads from the CDN.
5. World: 64 x 64 cells, top at y = 0. Collision uses a voxel grid (cell set), not physics meshes.
6. Rendering: one merged mesh with only visible cube faces. The first version (instanced cubes) ran at 1 fps in the software test browser.
7. Houses: 7 x 7 cells, 4 high, stepped roof. Door is 3 wide and 3 high so the player can go inside. 3 houses on each side of a main path. Each villager has one house (h1–h6).
8. Villager families: Oyunaa is Batbayar's daughter (mother Saruul Bold, not a villager). Tsetseg's mother is Enkhjargal. Ganbold Erdene and headmaster Batsaikhan Erdene are brothers (same father, Erdene Damdin). Birthdays are "day-month-year".
9. The player cannot move while a dialog or panel is open. Space only advances dialog then.
10. Quest data (`quests.json`) was generated by a script that uses `dates.js`, so every weekday is computed, not typed. Weekdays were also spot-checked against Python's calendar.
11. A quest's liar is the one whose weekday does not match the real date. Liars alternate between A and B.
12. (Changed on Day 9.) There is no calendar panel. The weekday question is 7 buttons with no hints. The player decides from the two claims. A wrong answer: Buu says "No." and waits. The C key does nothing. Wall calendars in kitchens are only decoration.
13. Buu's date trap is said in the case announcement (third line). F opens a weekday panel for that date. Correct = "You are right. I am a silly panda." Wrong = "No. Look again." F when nothing is wrong: "I said nothing wrong." Six traps use a villager birthday, so the Family Book can also prove Buu wrong.
14. Words: `quests.json` keeps 3–5 theme words per quest (they appear in the claims). Per Day 4, the words actually taught come from `words.json` at the current level. The quest's word count sets how many.
15. New words are taught in example sentences said by villagers A and B (word in bold), then tested after the penalty in a "Word check": Buu speaks the word, the player clicks the written word from 4 options. No translations anywhere.
16. Adaptive rules count every answer: weekday picks, trap answers and word-check picks. First-try or not does not matter; each wrong click counts as wrong.
17. "Next quest reuses only known words": when there are fewer known words than needed, new words are used.
18. When a level runs out of new words, words come from the next level up, then from lower levels, then known words.
19. Next case starts 5 seconds after "Correct. Next." Talking to Buu (E) repeats the current task or reopens a closed panel.
20. Save (localStorage): quests done, level, streaks, known words, villagers met, items taken, letters read, intro seen. A quest in progress restarts from its beginning after reload. All storage access is in try/catch; a broken save starts a new game.
21. Family Book also lists items taken and letters read (counts, not scores).
22. Night: 4 min day, 2 min night, 8 s dusk fade. Stars at night. Buu says when night starts and ends.
23. Headmaster: visible near the gate in the day. At night he walks a route through the path to each patrolled house door, waits 2 s, and carries a lantern light. Sight: 9 blocks, 50° each side, blocked by walls. Caught: one English line, teleport to the gate, 4 s grace time. He does not catch while a dialog or panel is open.
24. Headmaster adaptive: 3+ catches in the last 5 minutes → speed 2.2 → 1.4 and sight 9 → 5 (back to normal when it drops below 3). 10 minutes with no catch → one more house (max 6), then the 10-minute timer restarts.
25. Letters: one per house, on a table. In the day Buu says "Read it at night. Not now."
26. Pointer lock: Esc frees the mouse and pauses the game with the start screen. Opening a panel frees the mouse without pausing. Big mouse jumps right after lock are ignored (Chrome bug).
27. Buu: walks to 3 blocks from the player, hops when blocked, teleports when stuck 2 s or more than 16 blocks away.

## Day 6 — Visual upgrade and bigger village (user request)

28. The user asked for a 3D look like a realistic game, and a bigger game. They chose "build it in code" (no downloaded models) and said "not good enough" means more content.
29. Style changed from voxel blocks to smooth 3D: textured walls, wallpaper, tile and plank floors, tiled roofs, window frames with shutters. All textures are drawn in code on canvas (`textures.js`). No image files.
30. Units are now meters. The village is about 114 x 109 m (old: 64 x 64 blocks).
31. People: built from capsules and spheres with joints (hips, knees, shoulders, elbows), a face (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, ears), hair styles, and clothes (apron, skirt, glasses, cap, beard). They walk, breathe, blink, turn the head to the player, and move the mouth while talking.
32. Buu is now a round panda made from spheres.
33. Each house has 2 rooms: a kitchen (table with a checked cloth, chairs, cabinets, stove, fridge, lamp, wall calendar, letter) and a bedroom (bed, wardrobe, desk, nightstand, picture).
34. New places: school with a classroom (headmaster's day spot), market with 4 stalls, well, benches, lake with a dock and boat, farm with crops, scarecrow and barn, forge, bread oven, street lamps, a fence around the village, forest and mountains.
35. Light: real sun shadows, tone mapping, a gradient sky, sun and moon. A warm light turns on inside the house the player is in. Windows and lamps glow at night. The headmaster's lantern is a real light.
36. Collision: box colliders in a grid, no voxels. The player can step up onto low things (dock). Water and fences block walking.
37. Static scene parts are merged into one mesh per material, so the game stays fast.
38. New task: 12 lost things are hidden in the village and in two bedrooms. E picks one up. Buu says its English name. The Family Book counts them.
39. A wall calendar in each kitchen opens the calendar (E).
40. Shift = run. Walk 4 m/s, run 7 m/s.
41. Headmaster route uses the road at x = 1.8 so he walks beside the well, not through it.

## Days 7–10 (user goal)

42. This cloud session has no OPENAI_API_KEY, and its network blocks api.openai.com. `scripts/make_audio.js` is written and assigns all audio ids, but no mp3 files could be made here. Run it on a machine with the key. No fake or silent files were made.
43. Audio id = `<speaker>-<FNV-1a hash of speaker|text>` (`audioid.js`). The browser and the script compute the same id, so a changed line gets a new file.
44. `audio_manifest.json` lists the mp3 files that exist. The game only requests listed files, so missing audio never causes 404 errors. With no files, lines are silent and text still appears 1.5 s after the line starts.
45. Voices: Buu alloy; Batbayar onyx, Dulamsuren shimmer, Oyunaa nova, Enkhjargal coral, Tsetseg sage, Ganbold echo; headmaster ash; narrator, letters and word lessons fable. Speed = CONFIG.ttsRate (0.85).
46. All spoken lines now live in JSON: villager lines in villagers.json (the headmaster was added there with role "headmaster"), generic Buu lines in buu.json (lines with names are written out once per name), quest lines in quests.json, letters in letters.json, words and example sentences in words.json (`audio` = word, `sentenceAudio` = sentence).
47. Word lesson sentences are spoken by the narrator voice, not the villager, because each sentence has one recording.
48. `?fast=1` turns off shadows and lowers resolution. Only tests use it.
49. Tests serve three.js from node_modules (package.json devDependencies) because some machines cannot reach the CDN.
50. Day 8 replaces the word check in item 15. After the penalty, Buu says "Bring me the X." Three things appear on the plaza (X and two others). The player picks one up (E, it shows in the hand) and gives it to Buu (E on Buu). Wrong thing: Buu says "No." and repeats the request; the thing goes back. Right thing: "Correct. Next."
51. X is one of 20 things that have a 3D model and are words in words.json (cup, hat, ball, book, egg, fish, bread, shoe, pen, bag, apple, hammer, carrot, coin, pencil, kite, shell, basket, umbrella, potato). It is a quest's new word when possible, else a thing at or below the player's level, new words first.
52. Each give counts as one answer for the adaptive level (right = correct, wrong = wrong).
53. Day 9 "catch" = the player proves an adult lied and takes the penalty. Adults: Batbayar, Dulamsuren, Enkhjargal, Tsetseg, Ganbold, the headmaster. Oyunaa is a child and is not counted. Catch counts are saved.
54. 3 catches: a panel with 3 nicknames (in villagers.json). The panel cannot be closed without a choice. Buu confirms ("<nickname>. I will remember.") and every Buu line that names that adult uses the nickname from then on. Each such line has recorded nickname versions ("nick" field), made by scripts/nicknames.js.
55. Market: adults walk to the market from second 100 to 190 of each day, then walk home. 5 catches: that adult stays home for one game day (today if it is day, else tomorrow), and says "I cannot go to the market today." once. Game days are counted and saved.
56. Headmaster with 3+ catches bows (in the day) when the player comes within 4 m, at most every 8 s.
57. Wrong weekday buttons are not marked (no hints). Esc still closes the question; E on Buu asks it again.
58. Day 10: quests.json is made by `scripts/build_quests.js` (story data + date engine), then `scripts/nicknames.js`, then `scripts/make_audio.js --ids-only`. Hand edits to quests.json are lost when the builder runs again. Edit the builder.
59. 50 quests, 5 arcs of 10. Dates and wrong weekdays come from a seeded random generator, so the file is the same each time.
60. Line length is checked on every line in quests.json, including Buu lines, narrator lines, plain versions and nickname versions. Buu lines that name a person are short enough that a 2-word nickname still fits.
61. "Past tense" = a word in a fixed list of irregular past forms, or a word ending in "-ed" (except bed, red, need, feed, seed, speed, hundred, shed, weed). Rules are in grammar.js and used by the game and the checker.
62. Past tense and because/so lines carry a form ("past" / "cause") and a plain present-tense version. The game says the plain version until the form is unlocked. Past unlocks after 5 correct answers in a row while in arc 4 or later. because/so unlocks after another 5 in a row while in arc 5. Every answer counts (weekday, trap, giving the thing to Buu). Nothing on screen mentions grammar.
63. Word lessons follow the same rules: if an example sentence is longer than the arc allows, or uses a locked form, only the word itself is said.
64. "Words already in words.json" = every headword and every word of the example sentences (540 words). Names, nicknames and numbers are not counted. The checker requires at least 90% per quest; the lowest is 92.6%.
65. Each quest from the second one has a line where villager A names an earlier case ("Like Bread Case.", "Remember the Panda Lie? This is the same.").
66. Arc 3 uses the headmaster as villager A or B in every quest. Arc 5 uses him twice. He can be talked to in quests (E).
67. report.json: a web page served by `npx serve` cannot write files. At every quest end the game adds one entry to localStorage key "report.json" (the full JSON text). The tests save it to test-results/report.json. Entry: quest, title, arc, time, game day, right/wrong answers in the quest, level, known words, grammar unlocked, catches, nicknames.
